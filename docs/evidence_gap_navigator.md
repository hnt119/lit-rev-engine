# Evidence Gap Navigator v0.1 — development guide

This feature is a scoped development change. It is not included in the published Mac, Windows or source releases. It requires no model.

## Follow up an unresolved comparison cell

1. Open **Compare** and select an unresolved cell, or find it through **Needs review**. Choose **Investigate related literature**.
2. Write your own reason for investigating. Choose **Create investigation draft** to save the reason before closing the page. Creating the investigation retains the exact board definition, paper, field, submitted cell revision, saved draft, source-manifest identities and any separately labeled editor snapshot. It does not submit a cell or replace an existing reference.
3. Review and edit the search plan. The field question is context, not an invented PubMed constraint. When the original comparison came from an approved search plan, that exact plan can supply the starting concepts. Otherwise, enter a strategy yourself.
4. Save the plan and read the **exact executable PubMed query**, including date limits, sort and any unresolved/deferred constraints. Explicitly approve that saved revision. Editing the query requires saving and approving the new revision before execution.
5. In a configured live workspace, explicitly run a diagnostic or execute the approved search with a bounded result limit. Viewing, reopening, filtering and returning do not call PubMed. Saved-example workspaces support drafting and audit inspection but cannot execute supplied queries.
6. Treat returned records as candidates. Choose **Shortlist**, **Dismiss** or **Leave unassessed** yourself. These decisions remain separate from the comparison board; they do not establish relevance, eligibility, support or study independence.
7. Return to the original cell to continue your own work. Reopen the saved investigation to inspect its plans, approvals, executions and decision history, or download its readable audit export.

An unresolved cell is not evidence of a scientific research gap. **Not found in inspected material** does not mean absent from the whole paper. A new paper cannot supply missing facts about a different paper. Search matches and scores do not establish scientific support.

## Keep your earlier work and audit

The investigation stores follow-up results separately. It does not add papers to the board, prepare or replace sources, change notes or judgments, or submit a cell. Original source references remain bound to their original versions. An investigation can remain readable after the original board becomes historical.

A stale revision or two-tab conflict requires explicit reconciliation; keep your edits and reload the current saved state. A failed or interrupted provider request is not a zero-result search. Replaying the same request does not silently make another provider call; choose a new explicit attempt when appropriate.

Audit exports contain the researcher's reason, captured origin, exact saved query revisions and approvals, provider receipts and manual candidate decisions. They are private research records and are not portable access credentials or a complete application backup. Existing browser-session and private-directory protections still apply.

Real PubMed execution and researcher usefulness are separate from offline software validation; consult the [evaluation report](evidence_gap_navigator_evaluation.md).
