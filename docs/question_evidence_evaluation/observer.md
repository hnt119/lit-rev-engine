# Observer instructions — no fabricated results

Status: blank guide, not an observation record. Use the approved protocol and assigned condition/set. Do not coach button sequences, silently repair a participant's output, supply field answers, or infer a support judgment. Offer only the pre-agreed help, recording its exact category and time. A software-prepared example is labeled as such.

Record only what occurs: task start/end, deliberate search/diagnostic/index actions, waits, edits, errors, help, corrections, source recovery, export/reopen and withdrawal. Record frustration or confusion as participant statements or observed actions, not a diagnosis. Do not fabricate timestamps, participant behavior or feedback. Keep software automation logs separate from human sessions.

Keep setup/install, cold model/index work, provider wait, repeated-query wait and active review segments distinct. Include correction/assistance time in the preregistered end-to-end measure; report any alternative measure separately. A missing event or abandoned condition remains missing/incomplete, never zero seconds.

Before leaving a condition, retain its permitted local output and source/version manifest, tool configuration, unresolved answers and actual access limitations. Never save keys, browser cookies, SSO details or private PDFs in the kit. A spreadsheet-only comparator may use its normal citations; do not require it to imitate the application's interface.

Source checking is a later, independently documented review of the same provided documents and field definitions. Do not disclose held-out answers or seeds during performance. Do not silently exclude difficult cases from analysis. Record protocol amendments and failed/unsupported operations.
