# Participant information and neutral feedback

Complete the approved local participant-information process before using this sheet. Participation is voluntary; stopping or declining a tool does not constitute failure. Do not enter unpublished findings or third-party credentials into this kit. Recordings need separate explicit permission and a stated retention/deletion policy; none are assumed.

Participant code: ______  Protocol/version: ______  Consent recorded separately: ______

Before testing, record role, review experience, usual tools and prior familiarity without names or institutional identifiers in public reports. Follow the agreed task using each assigned tool. Ask for help when needed; the observer records it. You may stop. The prototype and comparator outputs require your judgment; they are not verified research conclusions.

After each condition, leave answers blank until actually given:

- What was unclear or unexpectedly difficult?
- Could you explain what was searched and what remained unresolved?
- Could you identify which paper and field you were editing?
- Could you recover the exact source and determine whether it supported the value?
- How did you handle missing information or different cohorts/specimens?
- What did you need assistance to complete?
- Would you voluntarily reuse this workflow, and for which task?
- What would prevent reuse?

Do not convert a single person's feedback into a market or model-quality claim. Keep direct quotations private unless separately permitted for publication.
